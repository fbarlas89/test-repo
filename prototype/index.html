Before you practise

Who is this adhan for?

Hayya has no likes, followers, rankings or scores, and it never uploads your voice. This short moment keeps practice pointed at Allah.

Your voice

Every pattern in Hayya moves to fit your voice, so there is no "right" note to reach. Pick one you could hold all day without strain. Lower is fine.
